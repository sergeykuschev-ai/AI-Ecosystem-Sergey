# VOZDOOH brand-story evidence — 2026-09-27

Purpose: factual source notes for the customer-facing brand histories in src/catalog/landings.ts.
These notes are editorial evidence only. They do not alter 1C trade fields, prices, stock, product images,
product scent notes or recommendation relationships.

## Editorial rules

- Prefer the brand's own site; use institutional/industry sources where the official site omits a historical detail.
- Do not infer a founder when the brand does not name one publicly.
- Do not turn a brand-level material/process statement into a claim about every individual SKU.
- Product-specific notes and compatibility remain governed by the reviewed catalog evidence.

## CULTI MILANO

Sources:
- https://www.culti.com/en/our-world
- https://www.culticorporate.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/04/COMPANY_PROFILE_ENG_2020.pdf

Supported: Alessandro Agrati; research linking design and senses began in 1988; Culture of Ambience;
milk-bottle inspiration for the diffuser; work with master perfumers; the company's historical claim that
CULTI pioneered rattan-stick diffusion.

## TEATRO Fragranze Uniche

Sources:
- https://fragranze.pittimmagine.com/it/pittimmagine/archive/fragranze20/exhibitors/T/teatro-fragranze-uniche
- https://teatrofragranzeuniche.it/it/progetti-su-misura
- https://teatrofragranzeuniche.it/it
- https://teatrofragranzeuniche.ru/about-us

Supported: Florence, 2012, founded from the idea/dream of three women; names Fiorella Lucci,
Federica Schirra and Gaia Caiani are stated by the brand's Russian representation; master perfumers,
raw-material selection, internal laboratory, in-house production-chain supervision and Made in Italy.

## Lothantique

Sources:
- https://www.lothantique.com/la-marque-fr
- https://www.lothantique.com/notre-blog/lothantique-les-secrets-de-la-creation-dun-parfum

Supported: 1920s origin near Montagne de Lure; Joséphine and Mouranchon Frères; essential oils,
lavender sachets, soaps and eaux de toilette; grandchildren Domy and Denis Vogade took over and
renamed the house Lothantique in 1987; traditional know-how combined with modern methods.

## Christian Tortu

Sources:
- https://www.rometti.it/en/people/christian-tortu/
- https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christian_Tortu
- https://www.fragrantica.com/news/Pitti-Fragranze-2019-New-Riviera-Cologne-by-Christian-Tortu-12776.html

Supported: Christian Tortu's farming/gardening childhood in Anjou; florist and designer; Paris career
and own boutique; naturalistic floral language using flowers, branches, fruits/vegetables and greenery;
home fragrance as an extension of the same nature-led world. Exact fragrance ingredient claims are
not made at brand level.

## VELLUTIER

Sources:
- https://vellutier.pl/do/informacje/marka-vellutier
- https://vellutier.pl/
- https://vellutier.com/vellutier.pdf

Supported: current official positioning as a Polish premium candle brand; Jewellers of Fragrance;
rapeseed-based wax blend with purified paraffin and microcrystalline wax; dedicated glass and solid-wood
lid; layered perfume compositions. The official brand story does not identify an individual founder, so
none is named on VOZDOOH.

## DANHERA Italy

Sources:
- https://www.danhera.com/it/img/cms/Disciplinare_eng.pdf
- https://fragranze.pittimmagine.com/en/pittimmagine/archive/fragranze20/exhibitors/D/danhera-italy

Supported: founder Daniela Ciaffardoni / Daniela C.; brand-name connection Daniela + Hera; Marche /
Porto Sant'Elpidio; Italian design/formulation/production; long research background, selected/raw
materials and brand emphasis on high-quality ingredients. Do not transfer Home Purity detergent
ingredient restrictions to fragrance SKUs.

## VINOVE

Source:
- https://vinove.pl/en/about-vinove-car-perfumes/

Supported: international team of French perfume creators, Italian designers and Polish scientists;
recipes and fragrance essences developed in Grasse; Italian design; fragrance polymer technology;
Polish production. No individual founder is claimed.

## Millefiori Milano

Sources:
- https://millefiorimilano.com/pages/brand

## Castelbel

Source:
- https://castelbel.com/en/pages/sobre-a-castelbel

Supported: founded by Aquiles Barros in 1999 in Castêlo da Maia; name origin Castel + Bel; artisanal
craftsmanship beginning with soaps and hand-wrapping; later expansion into diffusers, candles and
fragrant home/body products; Made in Portugal identity.

## Ladenac Milano

Source:
- https://www.ladenac.com/en/about-us

Supported: Patrick Douenat as the brand's central creative figure with 30+ years in luxury/perfumery;
Milan as aesthetic reference, Madrid/Spain as company roots; materials/essences/textures as creative
inputs; proprietary formulations tied by the brand to family tradition since 1884 on the Mediterranean.
The page is careful to describe Patrick as the brand's alma mater; VOZDOOH therefore uses
creative direction, not an unsupported legal-founder claim.

## AROMAgroup

Sources:
- https://vologda.aroma-group.ru/company/production/
- https://www.retail.ru/rbc/company/aromagroup/

Supported: founded in 2005; professional scent marketing / room scenting; own fragrance and equipment
R&D; fragrance-development cooperation stated with Givaudan, Symrise, Takasago and MANE; testing and
certification; part of equipment production moved to Shenzhen in 2013 while preparation/service and
some advanced production remain in Russia. No individual founder is claimed.
