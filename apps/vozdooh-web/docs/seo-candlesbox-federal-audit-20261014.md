# Candlesbox vs VOZDOOH: Russia commercial SEO audit — 14 October 2026

## Executive decision

Protect currently working public indexing and commerce. Build federal demand around existing in-stock brands, category pages and real products. Candlesbox demonstrates useful category/brand/product architecture and purchase-oriented descriptions, but its markup and parameter policy are not a blueprint to copy blindly. No traffic, ranking, Wordstat volume, sales or indexation advantage is claimed.

Top priorities: (1) verify and contain index eligibility of internal debug views; (2) use existing brand/category/product landing pages instead of generating filter or city doorway pages; (3) reconcile delivery copy with active checkout, then improve collection schema and distinct page roles. The preceding federal homepage release is deployed and preserved by this audit.

**Work mode:** template sample plus sitemap/source inventory. **Market:** Russia, Russian language; federal commercial queries with a real Khabarovsk store/pickup location. **Engines:** Yandex and Google, assessed separately. **Evidence:** current HTTP response, raw HTML metadata/links/JSON-LD, repository source, mobile/desktop browser DOM; no private Webmaster/Search Console, Wordstat, analytics or authenticated crawl logs. Conversion objective: qualified browsing and purchase intent; the audit creates no orders, payments, delivery bookings or email.

## Yandex accessibility and robots/sitemap interpretation

Yandex-specific request evidence: all nine user-agent probes returned 200 with the expected public index controls; robots/sitemap access was checked on both sites (completion appendix). Engine-side rendering and authenticated crawl/index evidence are unavailable. A spoofed user-agent is only a request probe: it is not a verified robot IP, proof of successful crawl, or Webmaster URL inspection. No `site:` result count is used as index coverage. Authenticated Webmaster checks (server response, pages in search, excluded URLs, selected canonicals, crawl failures, regional settings) require owner access and are backlog items.

Current provider guidance reviewed on 14 October 2026: [Yandex robots.txt](https://yandex.ru/support/webmaster/ru/controlling-robot/robots-txt), [Yandex meta directives](https://yandex.ru/support/webmaster/ru/controlling-robot/metatags), [Google canonicalization](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/consolidate-duplicate-urls), [Google pagination](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/ecommerce/pagination-and-incremental-page-loading). Robots restricts fetching, meta noindex is read after fetching, canonical indicates a preferred representation, and sitemap declares discovery intent. None proves inclusion or rankings. Avoid adding Clean-param to content-changing filters: the competitor rule is an observed configuration, not evidence that it is suitable for VOZDOOH.

### UX and search intent interpretation

Candlesbox's category structure emphasizes format, brand, aroma/size filters, a category introduction, product names containing format/volume/brand and conventional paged browsing. VOZDOOH emphasizes curated merchandising, visible brand/format context, product-specific original/Russian names, product facts, related collections and selection guides. Both expose actual product URLs in initial HTML. VOZDOOH's broad catalog deliberately differs from a tight format landing; direct links to existing `/categories/diffusers` and `/categories/candles` better serve exact format-shopping intent than duplicating the full catalog. The gift-diffusers page addresses choosing a gift rather than asserting gifts are wrapped or selling best.

Product heading length and title character counts are not ranking pass/fail rules. A concise fragrance H1 is understandable when brand, format/volume, image, price and actual availability are present beside it. Improvements should help users distinguish fragrance, format, size, manufacturer-supported scent facts and delivery options; they must not infer stock, originality certification, free nationwide delivery or sales rank. The active cart/search/homepage changes belong to the preceding releases and require post-release verification before this audit is finalized.
