# Local SEO V1.1 — landing structure without a catalog

Date: 2026-09-10
Scope: `apps/stores-web` only. No Directus schema, data, or production changes.
Status: implemented (this document records what shipped and which commercial facts still require an owner decision).

## Goal

Strengthen relevance for Amursk local-intent searches around the four stores
(«Ампер», «Вентиль», «Метиз Маркет», «Миска») without a product catalog and
without inventing assortment, prices, brands, guarantees, or service claims.

## Verified fact inventory

Only the following facts were reused. Sources: `lib/data/mock-data.ts`,
`docs/DATA_MODEL.md`, brand landing pages (`app/<brand>/page.tsx`).

- City: Амурск, Хабаровский край, Россия (`cities`, slug `amursk`).
- Address of all four stores: г. Амурск, проспект Победы, 16.
- Opening hours (common): Пн–Пт 09:00–19:00, Сб–Вс 09:00–18:00.
- Per-brand telephones (`storeTelephoneByBrandSlug` in mock data).
- Brand directions (short descriptions and category names in mock data):
  - «Ампер» — электротовары, товары для электромонтажа, освещение, электроинструмент, расходные материалы.
  - «Вентиль» — сантехника, водоснабжение, отопление, арматура, смесители, канализация, расходные материалы.
  - «Метиз Маркет» — крепёж, саморезы, болты, гайки, анкеры, дюбели, инструмент, расходные материалы.
  - «Миска» — корма, лакомства, наполнители и туалеты, уход, игрушки, амуниция, витамины и добавки, паразитарные средства.
- Bonus program rules: earn rate, spend cap, validity, card issue thresholds
  (`lib/constants/bonus.ts`), referenced on every brand page.
- «Ампер» works in Amursk since 2018 (already published on `/amper/`).
- «Миска» stocks AWARD Veterinary Diet (already published on `/miska/`).

## What changed

Content and internal linking (no new routes, no URL changes, sitemap unchanged):

1. `lib/data/mock-data.ts` — four brand-scoped local-intent FAQ records
   («Где купить … в Амурске?»), answers limited to the verified address,
   directions, and a pointer to the store page. `brand_id`/`city_id` set.
2. `app/page.tsx` — home «Частые вопросы» now shows general FAQs only
   (brand-scoped ones stay on `/faq/`) and links to `/faq/`.
3. `app/faq/page.tsx` — «Полезные разделы» block linking to `/kontakty/`,
   `/stores/`, `/bonus/`; all four local-intent FAQs render here and in FAQ
   JSON-LD.
4. `app/stores/[city]/page.tsx` — «Направления магазинов» section on the city
   page links each brand present in the city to its landing page with the
   verified short description.
5. `app/stores/[city]/[store]/page.tsx` — every store page now links back to
   its brand landing page (`/<brand>/`), closing the brand ↔ store loop.
6. `scripts/directus/seed.ts` — FAQ seed preserves `brand_id` scope (parity
   between mock content and Directus; the seed script is run manually and was
   not executed against any database for this change).
7. `tests/local-seo.test.ts` — regression tests: local-intent FAQ coverage per
   brand with verified facts only, separation of general/brand FAQs, unique
   metadata titles/descriptions across static pages, store→brand link contract.
8. `.github/workflows/stores-web-ci.yml` — added `git diff --check` and a
   changed-files secret-pattern scan to the existing test/lint/typecheck/build
   pipeline.

## Deliberately excluded (owner decisions required)

The following phrases/claims would imply unverified commercial facts. They are
NOT used anywhere in V1.1. Each needs an explicit owner decision and a
verifiable source before being added:

- Prices, discounts beyond published promotions, «низкие цены», «лучшие цены».
- Assortment depth claims: «широкий/самый большой ассортимент», «всегда в
  наличии», stock or availability statements.
- Product brands and manufacturer names (except AWARD Veterinary Diet for
  «Миска», already verified). No other trademarks may be named.
- Guarantees, return policy, service claims: «гарантия», «возврат», «доставка
  по Амурску», «установка», «монтаж», «выезд на замер».
- Any second address, coordinates, or opening-hours deviation — only
  проспект Победы, 16 and the common hours above are verified.
- Legal entity names (`legal_name` is empty in the data model).
- Aggregated ratings, reviews, price ranges in JSON-LD (already guarded by
  tests; do not add).

## Guardrails kept

- No new pages per category: category names render as navigation aids only,
  not as thin doorway pages. Category landing pages wait for the future
  catalog stage (see README «Future catalog»).
- Titles/descriptions stay unique per page (enforced by a regression test).
- Canonical URLs, trailing-slash policy, robots, and sitemap unchanged.
- No e-commerce, stock, price, or catalog features; no Directus/DB changes;
  no production deploy from the worker environment.
