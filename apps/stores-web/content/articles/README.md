# Statejnik article drafts

This directory is the review boundary for the AmurskMarket content pilot.

Rules:
- Statejnik may create research and draft material, but a draft is not a public page.
- A human-reviewed article must be explicitly added to `lib/articles/articles.ts` before it becomes indexable.
- Never invent prices, stock, addresses, product specifications, promotions, licences, guarantees or medical claims.
- Cite verifiable primary/authoritative sources in research notes.
- Prefer useful local intent over search-volume padding.
- Initial pilot limit: 5–10 reviewed articles.
- Do not submit IndexNow or Yandex recrawl until an article is approved and deployed.
